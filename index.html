Pawmodoro Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

In short: Pawmodoro does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app doesn't even have internet access. Everything you do in the app stays on your phone.

This policy explains how the Pawmodoro app for Android (package app.pawmodoro) handles information. Pawmodoro is a free Pomodoro focus timer where you adopt and dress up cats.

Information we collect

None. Pawmodoro has no sign-up, login or account, and it does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send any information to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following only in its private storage on your phone:

  • the cats you adopted, the names you gave them and their outfits;
  • your completed focus sessions (start time, end time and length), used for your stats and streaks;
  • which clothes and cats you have unlocked;
  • your settings (timer lengths, sound, vibration, notifications and similar) and the current timer.

We cannot see or access this information. You can delete all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app.

Android backup

If you have turned on backup on your phone, Android may include Pawmodoro's data in your device backup to your own Google account, so your cats come back when you switch phones. This backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings and Google's Privacy Policy. We cannot access it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): shows the countdown while a session runs and tells you when time is up. You can turn notifications off in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • Alarms (USE_EXACT_ALARM, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): makes the "time's up" alert arrive on time, even when the app is closed.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): restores a pending "time's up" alert after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE) and keep awake (WAKE_LOCK): vibrate when a session ends and optionally keep the screen on while you focus.

None of these permissions give the app access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera or any other personal data.

Links to other services

From the Settings screen you can open this policy in your browser, the app's Google Play page to leave a rating, and the original design files on Figma. These open outside Pawmodoro and are governed by the privacy policies of those services.

Children

Pawmodoro collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
